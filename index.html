How AGRI-XCHANGE works

  1. Farmer selects a preferred language and reports a crop problem.
  2. The system creates a unique Problem ID.
  3. Students see the problem in their selected language and submit solutions in their own language.
  4. AGRI-XCHANGE translates stored messages for the person viewing them.
  5. Other students can vote on useful solutions.
  6. A professor reviews, verifies or rejects a solution.
  7. The farmer sees the verified solution in the farmer's selected language and can rate it.

Important: automatic translation is an assistance layer. Agricultural recommendations should be checked by a qualified professional before field use.
