AGRI-XCHANGE

A community-based agriculture platform where farmers can share crop problems and receive solutions from students, with professor verification and community feedback.

Who Can Use AGRI-XCHANGE?

The core user roles of the AGRI-XCHANGE concept

👨‍🌾 Farmer

Shares crop problems, photos and relevant information and can provide feedback on solutions.

🎓 Student

Can contribute agriculture-related solutions and participate through community voting.

👨‍🏫 Professor

Reviews submitted solutions and provides verification before a solution receives final status.

Students can view farmer problems and suggest agriculture-related solutions.

🔄 How AGRI-XCHANGE Works

1️⃣ Farmer Reports Problem

Farmer enters crop details, problem description, voice information and photo.

2️⃣ Problem Gets an ID

Each submitted problem can be identified using a unique problem ID.

3️⃣ Students Contribute Solutions

Students can provide possible solutions related to the reported problem.

4️⃣ Evidence Can Be Added

Field photos and bill photos can support the submitted solution.

5️⃣ Farmer Rating & Student Votes

Community feedback can help evaluate submitted solutions.

6️⃣ Professor Verification

A professor can review the solution and provide verification.

7️⃣ Final Status

After the review process, the solution can receive its final status.
