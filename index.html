How AGRI-XCHANGE Works

1️⃣ Farmer Reports

Farmer submits crop details, text and optional media.

2️⃣ Privacy Choice

Farmer chooses whether to share the problem.

3️⃣ Students Help

Students can view shared problems and suggest solutions.

4️⃣ Language Translation

The original text remains available; translation is best-effort.

5️⃣ Professor Review

The professor reviews submitted student solutions.

6️⃣ Verification

The professor marks a solution Verified or Rejected.
