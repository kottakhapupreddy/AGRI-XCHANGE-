AGRI-XCHANGE

A collaborative agriculture platform where farmers share crop problems, students provide possible solutions, and professors verify the solutions before they are considered final.

🎓 Student

👨‍🏫 Professor

Professors can review submitted problems and student solutions and verify or reject solutions.

How AGRI-XCHANGE Works

1️⃣ Farmer Reports

Farmer submits crop name and problem details. A unique Problem ID is generated automatically.

2️⃣ Students View

Students can view available farmer problems and select a problem using its Problem ID.

3️⃣ Students Suggest

Students submit possible agricultural solutions. Each solution is stored separately.

4️⃣ Professor Reviews

Professor checks the farmer problem and submitted student solutions.

5️⃣ Verification

Professor can verify or reject a student solution.

6️⃣ Farmer Feedback

Verified solutions can later be used for farmer feedback and rating.
