How AGRI-XCHANGE Works

1️⃣ Farmer Reports

Farmer selects a language and submits a crop problem. A unique Problem ID is generated.

2️⃣ Students View

Only farmers who select Allow Sharing will appear to students.

3️⃣ Students Suggest

Students submit possible agricultural solutions in their preferred language.

4️⃣ Translation

Original text is preserved and translated for the viewer's selected language.

5️⃣ Professor Reviews

Professor reviews the problem and student solution.

6️⃣ Verification

Professor verifies or rejects the solution.
