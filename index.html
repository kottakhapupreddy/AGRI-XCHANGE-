🔄 How AGRI-XCHANGE Works

1️⃣ Farmer

Farmer reports crop problem using text, voice and photo.

2️⃣ Sharing

Farmer chooses whether the problem can be shared with students.

3️⃣ Student

Students view shared problems and submit possible agricultural solutions.

4️⃣ Professor

Professor reviews and verifies or rejects student solutions.

5️⃣ Notifications

Users receive in-app notifications when important actions happen.
