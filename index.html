How AGRI-XCHANGE Works

1️⃣ Farmer Reports

Farmer selects a preferred language and submits crop problem, photo and voice description.

2️⃣ Privacy Choice

Farmer chooses Allow Sharing or Decline Sharing.

3️⃣ Students Help

Only shared problems are shown to students.

4️⃣ Language Translation

Original text remains visible and a translation is provided in the viewer's selected language.

5️⃣ Professor Review

Professor reviews student solutions.

6️⃣ Verification

Professor verifies or rejects the solution.
